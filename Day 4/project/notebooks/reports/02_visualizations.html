Q1. Which city pays highest average salary? Trichy pays highest - Avg Salary 90000, Chennai lowest - 50625.

Q2. Age ku Salary ku relation iruka? Yes, positive correlation. Age increase aana Salary um increase aagudhu. Oldest employee (Age 60) ku highest salary 90000.

Q3. Which city has most employees? Chennai has most employees - 4 employees, Madurai 1, Trichy 1.

Final Report - Summary¶

3 Key Insights:

  1. Trichy pays highest - Avg 90000
  2. Chennai has most employees - 4, but lowest avg salary 50625
  3. Age increase aana Salary um increase aagudhu - positive correlation
